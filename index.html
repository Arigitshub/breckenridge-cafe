Amsterdam Vibe. Brooklyn Heart.

Located at 319 Wythe Avenue in the historic heart of Williamsburg, Breckenridge Green Café (BG Cafe) is a cozy sanctuary built for connection, relaxation, and wellness.

We pair premium, locally sourced coffees with a curated catalog of hemp-derived cannabinoids. Whether you're here to work, enjoy our tranquil garden patio with your furry friend, or relax with a premium pre-roll, we provide a warm space designed to interrupt the autopilot of daily life.
